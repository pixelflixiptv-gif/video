THE ELEVATOR - PART 1 (horror story, narration script)

[3:17 AM - YOUR HALLWAY]
Your building has six floors.
And one elevator.
At 3:17 in the morning, it dings on your floor.
But nobody pressed the button.

[3:17 AM - THE ELEVATOR]
The doors do not open.
They just ding. Again. And again.

[3:19 AM - YOUR PHONE]
Your phone lights up.
A text from Mom: Open the door. I forgot my keys.
There is only one problem.
Mom is asleep in the next room.

[KNOW THE RULES]
Rule one: never count the dings.
If it reaches three, it knows you are listening.
Rule two: a voice you love must answer a question only they know.

[3:24 AM - HALLWAY CAMERA]
You check the hallway camera instead.
The hallway is empty.
Then the knocking starts.

[3:26 AM - YOUR PHONE]
Your phone buzzes in your hand.
A text. From your own number.
It says: Why won't you open? I'm cold.

[RULE THREE]
Rule three: it ends when the elevator reaches the lobby.
Not one second before.
The knocking just started again.
Do you open the door... or check the camera?
